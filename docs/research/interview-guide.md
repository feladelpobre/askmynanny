# askmynanny interview guide

**Goal:** talk to **5 families** and **5 nannies or kasambahay** (10 to 15 minutes each) and run a short survey. This is the real evidence for the validation report, so write down what people actually say.

**Rules that make the answers useful:**
- Ask about **what happened**, not what they *would* do. "Tell me about the last time you hired a yaya" beats "Would you use an app?"
- **Don't pitch askmynanny** until the last question. If you explain the idea first, people just agree to be polite.
- Ask "Why?" or "What happened next?" at least once per answer.
- Write their **exact words** for the strongest quotes.
- Use **first names or initials only**. Don't record IDs, addresses or phone numbers.

---

## Opening (both groups)

> "Hi, I'm Feladel. I'm a student working on a project about how families in the Philippines find nannies and kasambahay, and how nannies find work. It takes about 10 minutes. There are no right or wrong answers, and I won't use your full name. Is it okay if I take notes?"

**Tagalog:**
> "Hi, ako si Feladel. Estudyante ako at may project ako tungkol sa kung paano naghahanap ang mga pamilya ng yaya o kasambahay, at kung paano naghahanap ng trabaho ang mga yaya. Mga 10 minuto lang ito. Walang tama o maling sagot, at hindi ko gagamitin ang buong pangalan mo. Okay lang ba na mag-notes ako?"

Then ask: **City / province?**

## Script A: Families (employers)

| # | Question | What it proves |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Do you have a yaya or kasambahay now, or did you in the last 2 years? How many have you hired in total? | Specific user |
| A2 | **Tell me about the last time you hired one.** Where did you look first: Facebook group, agency, referral, an app? | Current alternatives |
| A3 | How long did it take from starting the search to her first day? | Pain severity (time) |
| A4 | **What was the hardest or most worrying part?** Why? | Core problem |
| A5 | How did you check that she was who she said she was? Did you ask for an ID, NBI or barangay clearance, or call a past employer? What happened when you did? | Verification gap |
| A6 | **Has anything ever gone wrong?** No-show, left without notice, cash advance (vale) not paid back, missing items, experience that wasn't true. What happened, and what did it cost you? | Pain severity (money, safety) |
| A7 | Have you used an agency or a paid app? How much did it cost? Was it worth it? Why or why not? | Willingness to pay |
| A8 | Do you know the minimum wage for kasambahay in your area, and the benefits you owe (SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, 13th-month pay)? | Compliance awareness |
| A9 | If you could fix one thing about hiring a yaya, what would it be? | Priority |
| A10 | *(Now explain askmynanny in one sentence.)* "It's a site where every nanny and every family has verified their ID with a live photo, and families only contact verified nannies." **What would make you trust it, or not?** Would you pay for it, and how much? | Solution fit, willingness to pay |

**Closing:** "Can I contact you when there's a version you can try?" (Yes or no only. Don't write their number in the notes.)

## Script B: Nannies and kasambahay

Use Tagalog or Bisaya if she's more comfortable. Be gentle: some answers may be about bad experiences.

| # | Question (English / Tagalog) | What it proves |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | How long have you worked as a yaya or kasambahay? / *Gaano ka na katagal nagtatrabaho bilang yaya o kasambahay?* | Specific user |
| B2 | **How did you find your current or last job?** / *Paano mo nahanap ang trabaho mo ngayon o ang huli mong trabaho?* | Current alternatives |
| B3 | How long did it take to find it? / *Gaano katagal bago ka nakahanap?* | Pain severity (time) |
| B4 | **Have you seen a job offer that was fake, or that asked you to pay a fee?** What happened? / *May nakita ka bang pekeng job offer, o humingi ng bayad?* | Scams and fees |
| B5 | Did any employer ask you to pay for your NBI, police clearance or medical certificate? / *May employer bang nagpabayad sa iyo ng NBI, police clearance o medical?* | Who pays for documents |
| B6 | Were you paid on time and at least the minimum wage, with a weekly rest day? / *Nabayaran ka ba sa tamang oras, hindi bababa sa minimum, at may day off kada linggo?* | Fair treatment |
| B7 | **Before accepting a job, how do you check if the family is safe and real?** / *Bago ka tumanggap ng trabaho, paano mo nalalaman kung totoo at ligtas ang pamilya?* | Two-way trust |
| B8 | Do you have a smartphone and mobile data? Which apps do you use most (Facebook, Messenger, TikTok)? / *May smartphone at data ka ba? Anong apps ang madalas mong gamitin?* | Access and channel |
| B9 | *(Explain askmynanny in one sentence.)* "Families and nannies both verify their ID with a live photo, and it's free for nannies." **Would you do the ID check if it brought you more job offers? What would worry you?** / *Papayag ka bang mag-verify ng ID kung mas maraming job offer ang dadating? Ano ang ikinakabahala mo?* | Solution fit, privacy concerns |

**Closing:** "Salamat po! Is there anything else you want families to know about working as a yaya?" (Often the best quote.)

## Quick survey (Google Forms, 2 minutes)

Share it in Facebook mom groups, kasambahay groups, and with classmates and relatives.

1. I am a: **family hiring help** / **nanny or kasambahay**
2. City or province
3. How do you usually find help or work? (Facebook group / agency / referral from family or friends / app / other)
4. In the past 2 years, which happened to you? (pick all) No-show; left without notice; unpaid cash advance; fake profile or fake offer; asked to pay a fee; underpaid; none
5. How much do you trust Facebook groups for hiring or finding work? (1 = not at all, 5 = completely)
6. Would mandatory ID + live-photo verification for **both** families and nannies make you more likely to use a platform? (Yes / Maybe / No)
7. *(Families only)* Would you pay to contact verified nannies? (No / up to ₱300 / ₱300–700 / more than ₱700)
8. *(Optional)* Anything else? (short answer)

**Target:** 30+ responses. Screenshot the summary charts in Google Forms; they go into the validation report.
